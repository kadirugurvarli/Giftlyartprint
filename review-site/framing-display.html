1. Economy: wood frame and removable poster

The problem
You want something tidy on the wall without paying for glass and mounts.
What we offer
A ready-to-hang wood frame without glass, or a poster that can be removed later.
Who it suits
Bedrooms, offices, rentals, quick refreshes.
Included
Print, wood frame (black, white or oak) and backing.
Extra cost
Glass, mount, delivery and hanging fittings unless quoted.
What to do now
Send your image and size.

No glass means the printed surface is exposed. It can mark if touched, so it suits dry, low-traffic rooms. If you want protection, ask about a glazed frame under Special presentation.

2. Hang without drilling: SnappyTack magnetic frames

The problem
You rent, have plasterboard you do not want to drill, or like to swap pictures often.
What we offer
A magnetic frame system where the print is held by magnets, so changing pictures is quick.
Who it suits
Renters, offices, anyone who changes displays.
Included
Magnetic wooden frame (black or oak) with your print.
Extra cost
Wall fixings or adhesive if the system needs them, delivery.
What to do now
Tell us the wall type and the picture size.

Check your surface and conditions of use. No-drill hanging depends on the wall surface, paint, weight and how it is fitted. We explain suitable surfaces and limits before you buy. We do not promise damage-free removal from every wall.

3. Frameless: Dibond and Foamex / PVC board

The problem
You want a clean, frameless look, or a rigid board for a shop or event.
What we offer
Your print mounted on rigid board, ready to display with hanging fittings if needed.
Who it suits
Modern interiors, shops, exhibitions.
Included
Print mounted on board.
Extra cost
Frame, stand, hanging fittings unless quoted, delivery.
What to do now
Send the image, size and where it will be used.

Foamex / PVC board

Rigid, lightweight PVC board for signs, shop displays and events. Quoted on request.

Dibond

A rigid aluminium-faced board. The print is mounted on it. A firm, modern look with no frame.

Foamex / PVC board

A rigid, lightweight PVC board, popular for signs and displays.

Kapa-fix / foamboard

A different product: paper-faced foam board, lighter and more economical. Not the same as PVC board.
