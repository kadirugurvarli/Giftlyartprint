How a session works

  1. Message us

    Tell us who the session is for, how many people and what you would like to end up with.

  2. Choose a package

    Packages are based on how many irises are photographed, then print size and finish.

  3. The session

    Session length, place and preparation are confirmed when you enquire.

  4. Choose your pictures and products

    Digital files and physical products are separate choices. See the table below.

The problem
You want a personal, unusual portrait or gift that is not a standard photo.
What we offer
An iris photography session with a print or digital choice, for one person, a couple or a family.
Who it suits
Individuals, couples, families, gift givers.
Included
Session and the package you choose. Exact inclusions are to be confirmed by Giftly.
Extra cost
Extra irises, bigger sizes, other finishes, and digital files if not in your package.
What to do now
Message us with who it is for and a rough budget.
