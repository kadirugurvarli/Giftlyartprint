What would you like to do? What do you already have? How can we help? Those three answers matter more than the paper name.

  1. Send your image

    By WhatsApp or email, with where it will hang or be given.

  2. We check the file

  3. You get a clear quote

    Size, paper, any mount or frame, and the total, before anything is printed.

Common worries, answered

The problem
You are not sure your photo is sharp enough, or what size to print.
What we offer
We look at the file and recommend a print size, with a light clean-up where it helps. Some files will not suit large sizes, and we will tell you before you pay.
Who it suits
Phone photos, downloaded images, scans, family pictures.
Included
Checking your file and advising on size.
Extra cost
Detailed retouching or repair is quoted separately (see Photo Restoration).
What to do now
Send the image and your preferred size, or just where it will go.

Already have a frame?

Tell us the frame’s inside (opening) size, and whether it has a mount. We size the print to fit.

  • Exact fit: if your photo’s shape matches, we print to size.
  • Approved crop: if the shapes differ, we show you the crop and only print once you agree.
  • Added border: or we add a white border instead of cropping into the picture.

Please do not trim anything yourself. Measure the opening, not the outside of the frame.

Photo paper or Fine Art paper?

Both are printed in the studio. The difference is mainly the look and feel. Descriptions below are in plain terms; ask us for advice on your image.

Photo prints

The familiar photo look: smoother, with a lustre or gloss surface and strong colour.

  • Suit family photos, holidays and everyday prints.
  • Usually the more economical choice.
  • Papers listed in the shop include Hahnemühle Photo Luster 260gsm, Photo Glossy 260gsm and Matt Fibre 200gsm, and a Permajet gloss metallic 300gsm.

Fine Art prints

Heavier artist-style papers with a softer, matt or textured finish.

  • Suit black and white, portraits, artwork and anything you want to feel more like a gallery piece.
  • Cost a little more.
  • Papers listed in the shop include Hahnemühle Photo Rag 308gsm, German Etching 310gsm, Bamboo, Matt Baryta and Photo Rag Metallic 340gsm.

Paper descriptions are for review. Giftly to check the wording before launch.

Print border or mount? They are different things

Print border (margin)

A strip of blank paper around the picture, printed on the same sheet. It is part of the print, so the image prints smaller than the paper.

Mount (passe-partout)

A separate card with a window cut in it, placed between print and frame. It is an extra item, and it sits behind glass in a framed piece.
