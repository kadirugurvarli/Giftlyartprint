An acrylic block shows the photo with depth and stands by itself.

Sharp, well-lit photos. Send it and we will tell you honestly.

The problem
You want a personal gift quickly that does not need hanging.
What we offer
Your photo printed and set in a clear block.
Who it suits
Birthdays, anniversaries, Mother’s and Father’s Day, desks and shelves.
Included
