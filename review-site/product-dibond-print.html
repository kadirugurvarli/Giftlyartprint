Framing & Display · Frameless

Dibond print

Your print mounted on rigid Dibond (aluminium composite) board: a clean, frameless, modern look that hangs flat.

Message us on WhatsAppEmail instead

Does this sound like you?

I do not like frames. What else is there?

Dibond: the print is mounted straight on a rigid board, with no frame.

Will it bend or curl?

It is a rigid board, so it stays flat.

Is it protected by glass?

No. Handle and clean it gently.

Is Dibond the same as Foamex?

No. Dibond has an aluminium face. Foamex is PVC. See the Foamex page.

The problem, and our answer

The problem
Frames can feel heavy or fussy, and bare prints curl.
What we offer
A paper print mounted on Dibond with a choice of papers.
Who it suits
Modern interiors, shops, exhibitions, anyone wanting a frameless look.
Included
Print mounted on 3mm Dibond.
Extra cost
Frame, delivery, special hanging fittings unless quoted.
