Framed fine art print

A Hahnemühle fine art print in a quality wood frame, for work you want presented at its best. Mount and glass options are discussed with you.

Does this sound like you?

My photo is special. How do I present it properly?

A fine art paper, a wood frame, and if you want it, a mount and glass.

What is the difference between a print border and a mount?

A border is printed on the paper. A mount is a separate card window between print and frame.

Which paper should I choose?

Tell us the picture and the room. We recommend one; see Photo & Fine Art Prints for plain-English notes.

The problem, and our answer

The problem
A standard frame can make a favourite picture look ordinary.
What we offer
Fine art paper, black or oak wood frame, with mount and glazing quoted as options.
Who it suits
Portraits, black and white photography, artwork, special gifts.
Included
Fine art print and frame as listed.
Extra cost
Mount, glass and anything bespoke unless confirmed.
