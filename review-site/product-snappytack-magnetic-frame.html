Framing & Display · Hang without drilling

SnappyTack magnetic frame

A wood frame held on by magnets so your print is easy to swap, with no drilling for the picture itself. Check your wall surface first.

Does this sound like you?

Can I hang pictures without drilling?

That is what the system is designed for. It depends on the wall, so tell us what you have.

Will it damage my wall when removed?

We cannot guarantee damage-free removal from every wall. We explain suitable surfaces and limits before you buy.

Can I change the picture later?

Yes. The magnetic fixing makes swapping prints quick.

The problem, and our answer

The problem
Drilling is not allowed or not wanted, and you like to change pictures.
What we offer
A magnetic wooden frame that holds your print, with a fixing method suited to your wall.
Who it suits
Renters, offices, anyone who changes their display.
Included
Magnetic wood frame (black or oak) and your print.
Extra cost
Wall fixings if your wall needs them, delivery.
